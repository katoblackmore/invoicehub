How your client pays

Your client needs no crypto wallet and no crypto experience

A wallet is created for your client automatically, and only they have access to it. Your client buys crypto using their usual payment method through a licensed provider. After that, all that is left is to confirm the transfer.

invoice.py
import os
import requests

API_KEY = os.environ["INVOICEHUB_API_KEY"]

res = requests.post(
    "https://api.invoicehub.com/v1/invoices",
    headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {API_KEY}"},
    json={
        "amount": "50.00",
        "currency": "EUR",
        "client_email": "client@example.com",
    },
)
print(res.json())  # {'id': 'IH-4821', ...}

Frequently asked questions

Don't see the answer you're looking for? Get in touch.

General

Who are you?

InvoiceHub is a software service for issuing invoices and settling payments. Operator details are at the bottom of this page.

Which countries do you cover?

The service is currently available in selected European countries. The list is growing, contact us for the current one.

How much does it cost?

A fee is deducted from the invoice amount. Terms are agreed individually, get in touch.

Payments

What currency are invoices issued in?

In the currency you work in. The crypto equivalent is calculated at the exchange rate at the time of payment.

Does my client need a crypto wallet?

No. A non-custodial wallet is created automatically and only your client has access to it.

How does my client pay an invoice?

In crypto. If your client doesn't have the right crypto, they can buy it in a few steps with their usual payment method, through a licensed provider.

Do you hold my funds?

No. The transfer goes directly from your client's address to the address stated on the invoice.

What happens if my client does not pay?

The invoice stays open and reminders go out automatically. You can see this in the status.
